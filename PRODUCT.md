**Register:** product. This is a control-room tool; design serves the task.

**One line:** an autonomous inspection and triage console for a 500 MW block of
Bhadla Solar Park — it finds underperforming arrays, sends a drone to verify why,
and hands an operator a ranked, deadlined, approvable repair plan.

**Users:** a control-room operator at a desk or in front of a projector, dark room,
1920×1080 or 1366×768, under time pressure. Also: a hackathon judge, using the same
console, who has ninety seconds and will ask where every number came from.

**USP:** "the plan, not the picture." Competitors detect and report; this closes the
loop to an approved work order with a computed deadline, re-derives the plan live
under perturbation, and shows its arithmetic.

**Non-negotiable:** every number traces to the physics model or a cited primary
source. The ironbow thermal ramp is the signature colour language and is kept — it
is the same LUT the thermal camera uses, so the console and the camera speak one
language.
